Vinaya hair oil is made by steeping selected herbs and aromatic spices in coconut oil. Each one was chosen for a reason.

## Coconut oil: the tropical powerhouse

Rich in lauric acid, coconut oil can penetrate the hair shaft and help reduce protein loss. It is the base that carries every other botanical.

## Aloe vera: the soothing succulent

A familiar favourite in hair care, aloe vera helps hydrate and soothe the scalp and hair.

## Bhringraj: the king of hair

Known in India as Keshraj, or "King of Hair", bhringraj has been cherished for generations in hair and scalp care.

## Hibiscus: the flower of softness

Its leaves contain natural mucilage, giving this beloved hair-care flower its softening and conditioning character.

## Holy basil (tulsi): the sacred green leaf

A sacred and aromatic Indian plant, traditionally treasured for its cleansing and refreshing character.

## True cinnamon leaf: spiced leaves

While the bark is the familiar kitchen spice, its aromatic leaves are traditionally valued for their natural antimicrobial properties.

## Curry leaves: leaves of nourishment

Packed with antioxidants and plant nutrients, curry leaves support nourished hair and natural shine.
