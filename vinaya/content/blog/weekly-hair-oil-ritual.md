A good ritual is simple enough to repeat. Use Vinaya hair oil two to three times a week, continuously, and give your hair time: noticeable changes may take three months or longer.

## 1. Apply and comb

Take 1–2 ml in your palm. Apply gently to the scalp and hair lengths. Use your wooden comb to spread the oil evenly and remove excess.

## 2. Massage and relax

Gently massage your scalp with your fingertips for 5–6 minutes. Then leave the oil on for 20–30 minutes before washing.

## 3. Wash

Wash away with your favourite shampoo and towel dry.

## Can I leave it overnight?

No. Twenty to thirty minutes is enough. Wash away the remaining oil with your favourite shampoo.

## Patch test first

Check the ingredients for known allergies. Apply a small drop to your inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, wash off and do not use. Natural does not always mean allergy-free.
