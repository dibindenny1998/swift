Some things cannot be hurried. Vinaya hair oil follows a time-honoured preparation where herbs and spices are slowly cooked in pure coconut oil, the way it has long been done in homes across Kerala.

## Three simple steps

1. **Selected herbs and spices.** Every batch begins with botanicals chosen for the role they play: aloe vera, bhringraj, hibiscus, holy basil, true cinnamon leaf and curry leaves.
2. **Slowly cooked in coconut oil.** The botanicals are steeped in pure coconut oil over gentle heat, so the oil takes on their character.
3. **Filtered and bottled with care.** The oil is filtered and bottled, ready for your ritual.

> What begins in nature should not lose its connection to nature along the way.

## Why it matters

There are no shortcuts in this process, and nothing artificial added to hide them. The result is an oil that carries the natural aroma of its botanicals and coconut oil, with no artificial fragrance and no preservatives.

It is a small way of keeping a traditional practice alive: understanding where each ingredient comes from, and respecting the knowledge behind it.
