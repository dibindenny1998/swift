Every Vinaya Gift Set includes a wooden comb. It is part of the ritual: you use it to spread the oil evenly through your hair and remove any excess.

## Can I use it every day?

Of course. Smooth, rounded edges feel gentle on your hair and scalp, while being kinder to the planet. Carry it with you.

## Keeping it clean

- Wipe it clean after use.
- If needed, rinse quickly with mild soap and water.
- Do not soak it.
- Dry thoroughly before storing.

A wooden comb looked after this way will stay smooth and pleasant to use for a long time. Spread greener tomorrows.
