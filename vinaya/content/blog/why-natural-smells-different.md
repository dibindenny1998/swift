The first thing many people notice about Vinaya hair oil is its scent. It does not smell like the products we have become used to, and that is on purpose.

## An aroma that comes from the ingredients

Vinaya has a natural botanical aroma of green leaves, coconut, herbs and spices. It is not designed to smell like artificial perfume. There is no added fragrance: what you smell is simply what is inside.

## No artificial fragrance, no preservatives

Leaving out artificial fragrance and preservatives keeps the oil close to its source. It also means the aroma may feel more earthy and herbal than you expect.

## Getting used to natural

Like the taste of food made from scratch, a natural aroma can take a little getting used to. Many people come to prefer it: it is honest about where it comes from.
