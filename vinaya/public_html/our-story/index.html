Our Story

Rooted in a simple belief.

Nature has always been a part of how we care for ourselves.

I

Nature inspires us

A simple belief, rooted in nature.

Across generations and cultures, nature has inspired the ways people care for themselves.

We began with a simple belief: that the wisdom found in nature and traditional personal care deserves a place in modern life.

II

Tradition guides us

Keeping traditional wisdom alive.

Traditional personal-care knowledge is a valuable part of our shared heritage.

At Vinaya, we seek to honour this knowledge by carrying forward time-honoured ingredients and practices, thoughtfully adapting their inspiration for today’s world.

III

You are at the heart of what we do

The best of both worlds.

We want you to experience the goodness of traditional personal care with the convenience of modern living.

Every Vinaya product is developed with care, keeping your everyday needs in mind, bringing thoughtful personal care from its natural inspiration to your home.

The value lies not only in the ingredients, but in understanding where they come from and respecting the knowledge behind them.
