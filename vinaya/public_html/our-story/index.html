Our Story

Rooted in a simple belief.

Nature has always been a part of how we care for ourselves.

I Heritage

Across India, generations have used natural ingredients and traditional knowledge in everyday beauty and personal care.

II Change

Modern lifestyles brought convenience, but moved us away from many of these practices.

Much of that knowledge still lives in homes, gardens and local communities.

III Reconnection

Vinaya is about finding that connection again.

Bringing together natural ingredients, traditional knowledge and time-tested practices, in a way that feels relevant to life today.

The value lies not only in the ingredients, but in understanding where they come from and respecting the knowledge behind them.

IV From the Source to You

Vinaya aims to stay close to the source.

To understand the ingredients, to respect the knowledge behind them, and to keep the journey to the customer clear.

What begins in nature should not lose its connection to nature along the way.

V The Vinaya Approach

Personal care inspired by nature and tradition.

Bringing the best of nature back into everyday life with simplicity, care and purpose, from hair and skin to other forms of personal care.

A Way of Being.
