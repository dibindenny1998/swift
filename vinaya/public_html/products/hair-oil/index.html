Simple steps and natural care

Your Vinaya ritual

  1. 01

    Apply & comb

    Take 1–2 ml in your palm. Apply gently to the scalp and hair lengths. Use your wooden comb to spread the oil evenly and remove excess.

  2. 02

    Massage & relax

    Gently massage your scalp with your fingertips for 5–6 minutes. Leave for 20–30 minutes before washing.

  3. 03

    Wash

    Wash away with your favourite shampoo and towel dry.

Patch test first

Check ingredients for known allergies. Apply a small drop to your inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, wash off and do not use. Natural doesn’t always mean allergy-free.

Your questions, our answers

Why does Vinaya smell different?

Vinaya has a natural botanical aroma of green leaves, coconut, herbs and spices. It is not designed to smell like artificial perfume.

What is it used for?

For everyday hair care to nourish, condition and keep hair soft, smooth and healthy-looking. Suitable for all hair types.

Can I leave it overnight?

No. 20–30 minutes is enough. Wash away the remaining oil with your favourite shampoo.

Can I use the wooden comb every day?

Of course. Smooth, rounded edges feel gentle on your hair and scalp while being kinder to the planet.

How do I care for my comb?

Wipe clean after use. If needed, rinse quickly with mild soap and water. Do not soak. Dry thoroughly before storing.
