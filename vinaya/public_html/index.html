The goodness within

Botanicals at the heart of Vinaya.

Our first product steeps seven traditional botanicals in pure coconut oil. Watch them turn, and tap one to discover it.

Tap any ingredient to explore

The tropical powerhouse

Coconut Oil

Rich in lauric acid, can penetrate the hair shaft and help reduce protein loss.

The soothing succulent

Aloe Vera

A familiar favourite in hair care, helps hydrate and soothe the scalp and hair.

Spiced leaves

True Cinnamon Leaf

While the bark is the familiar kitchen spice, its aromatic leaves are traditionally valued for their natural antimicrobial properties.

The flower of softness

Hibiscus

Its leaves contain natural mucilage, giving this beloved hair-care flower its softening and conditioning character.

Leaves of nourishment

Curry Leaves

Packed with antioxidants and plant nutrients, curry leaves support nourished hair and natural shine.

The sacred green leaf

Holy Basil · Tulsi

Known as Tulsi, a sacred and aromatic Indian plant, traditionally treasured for its cleansing and refreshing character.

The king of hair

Bhringraj

Known in India as Keshraj, or “King of Hair”. Cherished for generations in hair and scalp care.
